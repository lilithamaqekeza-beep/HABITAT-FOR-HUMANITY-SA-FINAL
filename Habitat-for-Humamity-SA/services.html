Our Programmes

Habitat for Humanity South Africa works with communities and partners through different programmes that aim to improve housing conditions and strengthen communities.

The organisation focuses on housing, community development, volunteering, advocacy and partnerships to help create better living conditions.

Housing

Habitat for Humanity South Africa supports access to decent and affordable housing. The organisation works with communities and partners to help improve living conditions and create safe places for families to live.

Community Development

The organisation works with communities to support development and improve living environments. These activities help communities build stronger foundations and improve their quality of life.

Volunteering

Volunteers can contribute their time and skills to support the organisation's housing and community development activities. Their involvement helps communities work towards better living conditions.

Advocacy

Habitat for Humanity South Africa promotes awareness about the importance of decent housing. Its advocacy work encourages support for improved housing opportunities and stronger communities.

Partnerships

The organisation works with businesses, organisations and other partners to support its housing and community development initiatives. These partnerships help bring resources and support to communities.

Get Involved

You can support Habitat for Humanity South Africa by volunteering, becoming a partner or making an enquiry about its programmes.
