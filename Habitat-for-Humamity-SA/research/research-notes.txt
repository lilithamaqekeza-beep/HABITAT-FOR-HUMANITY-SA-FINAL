2. ABOUT THE ORGANISATION

Habitat for Humanity South Africa works with communities and partners
to improve access to decent housing and help address housing poverty.

The organisation brings together communities, volunteers, government,
private organisations and other partners to support housing solutions.

3. HISTORY

Habitat for Humanity South Africa began its work in South Africa in
1987.

4. MISSION AND VISION

The organisation's work focuses on helping people and communities
access decent housing and improving living conditions.

Its work is based on the idea that everyone should have a decent
place to live.

5. PROGRAMMES AND WORK

Habitat for Humanity South Africa works in areas related to:

- Housing
- Community development
- Affordable housing
- Advocacy
- Volunteering
- Partnerships

6. GA-RANKUWA HOUSING PROJECT

Habitat for Humanity South Africa worked with the City of Tshwane
and Nissan South Africa on a housing project in Ga-Rankuwa, Gauteng.

In 2021, 24 houses were handed over to first-time homeowners.
The project had previously resulted in additional houses being
handed over to families.

The project demonstrates the organisation's focus on decent housing
and working with partners and communities.

7. VOLUNTEERING

Volunteers can support Habitat for Humanity South Africa through
activities that contribute to housing and community development.

Volunteering provides an opportunity for people to become involved
in supporting communities.

8. WEBSITE PURPOSE

The purpose of the website is to provide visitors with information
about Habitat for Humanity South Africa, its work and programmes.

The website also allows visitors to make enquiries about
volunteering, sponsorship and general questions.
