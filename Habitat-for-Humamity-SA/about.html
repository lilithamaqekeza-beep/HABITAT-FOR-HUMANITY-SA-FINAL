About Us

Habitat for Humanity South Africa is a non-profit organisation that works with communities to improve access to decent housing.

The organisation brings people together to help create homes and stronger communities.

Our History

Habitat for Humanity South Africa began its work in South Africa in 1987. The organisation works with communities, government, private organisations and other partners to improve access to decent housing.

Our Mission

Habitat for Humanity South Africa brings people together to build homes, communities and hope.

Our Vision

Habitat for Humanity South Africa works towards a South Africa where everyone has a decent place to live.
